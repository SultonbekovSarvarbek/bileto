# Bileto Privacy Policy

Version dated [DD.MM.YYYY]

This Policy describes what personal data [Legal entity name], TIN [TIN], address: [address] ("Bileto", "we") collects when you use the Bileto mobile application and website (the "Service"), why we use it and how we protect it.

We process personal data in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" and other applicable legal acts. By using the Service, you consent to the processing of your data on the terms of this Policy.

---

## 1. What data we collect

**Account data**
- mobile phone number (for sign-in with an SMS code);
- the name you provide in your profile;
- your selected city and language.

**Seller verification data** (if you undergo verification)
- the result of identity verification through MyID: full name, date of birth, PINFL (personal identification number) and verification status. The photos and documents you show to MyID are processed by the MyID operator; we receive only the verification result and the data listed above.

**Transaction data**
- listings: event, section, row, seat, face value, price, comment;
- the **photo or screenshot of the ticket** uploaded for verification. It is visible only to a Bileto moderator and is not shown to Buyers;
- the ticket itself (file, QR code or barcode) transferred to the Buyer through the Service;
- purchase and sales history, statuses, disputes and the evidence attached to them;
- chat messages between the Buyer and the Seller.

**Payment data**
- for payments: payment method, amount, status and transaction ID. **The full card number and CVV are processed by the payment systems (Payme, Click, Uzcard, Humo); Bileto does not receive or store them**;
- for payouts to the seller: the masked number of the linked card (for example, 8600 •••• •••• 1234) and the payment system token.

**Technical data**
- device model, OS and application version, language, IP address;
- push notification token;
- error and in-app activity logs (for security and bug fixing).

**Photo access.** The application requests access to your photo gallery only so that you can select a screenshot of a ticket. We receive only the image you select.

## 2. Why we use data

| Purpose | Data |
|---|---|
| Sign-in and account protection | phone number, technical data |
| Carrying out a safe transaction: freezing money, transferring the ticket, payout to the seller | transaction data, payment data |
| Ticket verification and fraud prevention (counterfeit and resold tickets) | ticket photo, transaction history, verification data, technical data |
| Handling disputes and refunds | transaction data, chats, evidence |
| Notifications about sales, purchases, payouts and new tickets | phone number, push notification token |
| User support | all data relating to the request |
| Compliance with legal requirements (accounting and tax records, requests from government authorities) | transaction data, payment data |
| Improving the Service | anonymized usage statistics |

We **do not sell** your personal data and do not use it to show third-party advertising.

## 3. Who we share data with

- **The other party to the transaction** — only what is necessary for the transaction. The Buyer sees the Seller's name, rating, number of transactions and the "Verified" badge. The Seller sees the Buyer's name. Phone numbers are not disclosed to the parties.
- **Payment systems and banks** (Payme, Click, Uzcard and Humo processing centers) — to accept payments and process refunds and payouts.
- **MyID** — to verify the seller's identity.
- **SMS providers and push notification services** — to send codes and notifications.
- **Hosting and IT contractors** that process data on our behalf and are obliged to maintain confidentiality.
- **The event organizer** — if this is necessary to re-register a named ticket or to obtain a refund when an event is cancelled, and only with your knowledge.
- **Government authorities** — upon a lawful request, and also where fraud is suspected.

## 4. Where and how long data is stored

4.1. Personal data of citizens of the Republic of Uzbekistan is stored and processed on servers located in the territory of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

4.2. Retention periods:
- account data — for as long as the account exists and for [3 years] after its deletion (to resolve possible disputes);
- transaction and payment data — for the period established by tax and accounting legislation;
- ticket photos provided for verification — [90 days] after the event date;
- technical logs — up to [12 months].

Upon expiry of these periods, the data is deleted or anonymized.

## 5. How we protect data

- data is transmitted only over a secure connection (TLS);
- tickets and verification photos are encrypted at rest;
- employees access data only on a need-to-know basis, with access logging;
- card payment data is processed only by certified payment systems.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:
- find out what data of yours we process and obtain a copy of it;
- correct inaccurate data;
- withdraw your consent and delete your account;
- turn off push notifications in your phone settings;
- appeal against our actions to the authorized government body for personal data protection or to a court.

To do so, write to [privacy@bileto.uz] from the phone number linked to your account, or use the "Help" section in the application. We respond within [10] business days.

**Account deletion** is possible at any time in your profile or upon request to [privacy@bileto.uz]. If you have uncompleted transactions or payouts not yet received, the deletion will take place after they are completed. Data that we are required by law to retain (clause 4.2) is kept until the end of the established period.

## 7. Children

The Service is not intended for persons under 16 years of age. If we learn that we have received a child's data without parental consent, we will delete it.

## 8. Changes to the Policy

We may update the Policy. We will notify you of material changes in the application. The current version is always available in the application in the "Help & rules" section.
